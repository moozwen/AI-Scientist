Calibration note for this oracle: it is a PHREEQC thermodynamic equilibrium model calibrated against the 120 real measurements of Takaya et al. (2015). Its rank correlation with those measurements is rho ~ 0.72-0.77 across all conditions, which is lower than a trivial predictor that looks only at whether the acid is HCl or H2SO4 (rho = 0.818). It reaches rho = 0.876-0.929 only near 25 C; at 50 C and 75 C it generates a temperature dependence that is absent from the measurements, and the ranking inverts. It is blind to leaching time by construction, because an equilibrium model has no notion of time. Each score it returns carries in_domain, reliability and domain_note fields saying whether that particular condition is inside the calibrated regime.
